2. A guide, not a guarantee

Vantage describes places and predicts light, sun and moon positions, weather, sky conditions and tides. Predictions are estimates and can be wrong. Opening hours, fees, rules and access are checked but change without notice, so confirm before a special trip.

You are responsible for your own safety and for following the law. In particular:

  • Obey posted rules, closures and opening hours. Do not trespass. Respect private property and the privacy of people you photograph.
  • Get any permits you need, for example for commercial shoots, tripods in restricted areas, or drones.
  • Take care with tides, water, cliffs, weather, wildlife, traffic and darkness. Never rely on Vantage for your safety.

3. Community rules

Shots you add to a spot are visible to everyone using Vantage. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. When you post:

  • Post only photographs you took yourself.
  • Nothing offensive — no nudity, gore, hate, or harassment.
  • Keep it about the place: a shot from the spot, not an advert.
  • No people who would object to being posted, and no children.

Anyone can report a shot. Two independent reports hide it straight away, and a curator reviews every report, normally within one business day. You can also block a photographer: their shots disappear for you immediately. We remove content that breaks these rules, and we suspend or close the accounts of people who post it or abuse others. The same rules are in the app under Settings → Community guidelines.
